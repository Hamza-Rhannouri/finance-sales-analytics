# Financial Logic

Core calculations used in the project:

- Revenue = Price × (1 − Discount) × Sales
- Profit = Revenue − (Cost of Product × Sales) − Taxes − Shipping
- Margin % = Profit / Revenue
- Gross Profit = Revenue − COGS
- Gross Profit Margin % = Gross Profit / Revenue

For this project, COGS was defined using cost of product.

The financial formulas are documented explicitly so another analyst can understand the assumptions behind the dashboard rather than treating the measures as black boxes.
